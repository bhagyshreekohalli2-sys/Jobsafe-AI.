    🔎 Quick Safety Tips

    Never Pay to Get Hired

    Be cautious of requests for registration fees, deposits, or paid training to secure a job.

    Verify the Company

    Find the employer's official website and verify contact details independently.

    Protect Your Information

    Never share OTPs, passwords, or banking credentials with recruiters.
